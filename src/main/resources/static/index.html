About the College

Fuzhou Melbourne Polytechnic is one of only three Sino-foreign cooperative higher vocational institutions in China with independent legal-entity status, and the only one of its kind in Fujian Province. Approved by the Ministry of Education in January 2017, it builds on more than two decades of Sino-Australian cooperation in education.

Dual Qualifications

Students who meet both graduation requirements receive a Chinese college diploma together with an Australian Associate Degree or Diploma from Melbourne Polytechnic.

Australian Curriculum

Courses follow the century-old Australian TAFE model and the curriculum of Melbourne Polytechnic, adapted to local industry needs and delivered in a bilingual environment.

International Faculty

A bilingual, dual-qualified teaching team of Chinese and international lecturers supports learning that meets international professional standards.

Programs

Programs are grouped into electronic information, finance and trade, and tourism clusters, all aligned with internationally recognised qualifications.

Electronic Information

Computing, networking and digital technology programs that combine technical skills with international certification pathways.

Finance and Trade

International trade, business and finance programs, including a provincial-level high standard professional group in international economics and trade.

Tourism and Services

Hospitality and tourism programs designed around the growing service sector in Fujian and across the region.

Graduate Pathways

Three routes help graduates move from a vocational diploma to a degree and a career.

ROUTE 01

Employment

Strong English skills, international professional training and dual-industry mentors help graduates enter multinational and export-oriented companies.

ROUTE 02

Top-up Degree at Home

Graduates continue into bachelor programs at Chinese universities, with progression rates among the highest in the province.

ROUTE 03

Study in Australia

After 2.5 years in Fuzhou, eligible students can complete a bachelor degree in about one year at Melbourne Polytechnic, then a master degree in one more year.
